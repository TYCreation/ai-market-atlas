## Style Prompt

An executive AI market-intelligence film built from the existing AI Market Atlas visual identity: deep midnight navy, luminous cyan data signals, restrained orange alerts, ivory editorial typography, and structural market graphics. Motion should feel precise, analytical, and confident—more like a premium weekly research briefing than a trading-floor montage.

## Colors

- `#07121d` — primary midnight background
- `#0c1b29` — secondary panels and scene depth
- `#73e1e6` — primary data signal
- `#ff8a5c` — alerts, catalysts, and directional accents
- `#f4efe4` — primary text

## Typography

- `Geist` — headlines and explanatory copy
- `Geist Mono` — data, labels, dates, and market metadata

## Motion

- Medium-energy editorial pacing with confident `power3` pushes as the primary transition.
- One diagonal split marks the move from sector data into investable equities.
- A restrained blur crossfade introduces the closing thesis.
- Each scene builds, breathes, and resolves; ambient motion stays subtle and finite.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use purple gradients, generic neon glows, or trading-floor stock footage.
- Do not show six-panel dashboards, pie charts, chart-library visuals, or dense axis labels.
- Do not animate every element from the same direction or with the same easing.
- Do not use infinite loops, random values, or live network data.
- Do not turn the brief into buy/sell recommendations.
